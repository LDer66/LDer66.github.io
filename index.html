About Me个人简介

I am a faculty member at Guangxi Normal University, where I also conduct postdoctoral research. My research focuses on making machine learning more reliable and privacy-aware, with a particular interest in graph learning and federated learning.

我是李德，现为广西师范大学计算机科学与工程学院专任教师，同时开展博士后研究。我的研究主要围绕可信机器学习展开，重点关注图学习和联邦学习中的噪声标签鲁棒性与成员隐私保护。

I study robust learning under noisy labels and the interaction between model utility and membership privacy. I am also exploring physics-informed machine learning and distributed learning for scientific applications.

我的研究致力于提升噪声标签扰动下模型的分类性能，并探索模型效用与成员隐私保护的协同优化。同时，我也关注物理信息机器学习及其在分布式科学计算场景中的应用。

03Research Interests研究方向

Robust Graph Learning鲁棒图学习

Learning reliable graph representations in the presence of noisy labels and imperfect graph structures.

研究噪声标签与不完整图结构条件下的可靠图表示学习。

Federated Learning联邦学习
